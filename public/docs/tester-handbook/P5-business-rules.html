Business Rules & Expected Results

Where to find the business rules and the expected application behaviour for every module, and how they are written.

Scope: All modules of the website, Admin and CRM. The rules and expected results themselves live in the module test guides (00–25, W1–W5); this page is the index to them. About 900 test scenarios exist across the guides.

1. How each module guide is organised

Every module guide (01–25, W1–W5) follows the same eight sections, so a tester always knows where to look.

SectionWhat it holdsUse it for
  1. What this module does
Purpose, screens and their URLsUnderstanding the module before testing
  1. How it works (business rules)
Statuses, calculations, limits, automatic jobs, notificationsThe business rules to verify
  1. Who can do what
Action × role table (Super Admin, Agency, Agent)Role and permission tests
  1. Fields and validation
Inputs, limits, exact validation messagesNegative and boundary tests
  1. Test scenarios
ID, scenario, priority, steps, expected resultTest case execution
  1. Expected results checklist
Short list of what must hold trueSign-off and regression
  1. Known behaviour (not bugs)
Behaviour by design, test data cautionsAvoiding false bug reports
  1. Reporting a bug
What to include in a bug reportBug reports

Priority in section 5: High = core flow, data loss or access; Medium = important but with a workaround; Low = cosmetic.

4. Writing new expected results

When a module changes, update its guide in the same structure: add the rule to section 2, the role change to section 3, the message to section 4, and at least one scenario to section 5 with a quoted expected result (exact toast, message or status label).
